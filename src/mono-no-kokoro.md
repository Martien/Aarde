# {mono no kokoro}
{mono no kokoro} (物の心) translates to “the heart/spirit of things” or the ”mood of things,” referring to a deep, empathetic appreciation for the intrinsic nature, pathos, and transient beauty of the world. It is closely related to {mono no aware}, representing a profound emotional connection and resonance with objects or nature.

**Key Aspects of Mono no Kokoro:**

- **Understanding the Essence:** It implies understanding the true, often subtle, character of an object, moment, or creature, notes [The University of Arizona](https://communitycollegeoutreach.arizona.edu/sites/communitycollegeoutreach.arizona.edu/files/instructional/5%20Aesthetic%20Characteristics-%20Mono%20No%20Aware.pdf).
- **Sympathetic Resonance:** It is the feeling of being moved by the ephemeral nature of reality, acknowledging that the beauty of things lies in their impermanence, as explained by The University of Arizona.
- **Context:** While {mono no aware} (物の哀れ) focuses on the “pathos of things,” {mono no kokoro} focuses more on the inherent “spirit” or “mood” that causes that emotional response. 

In essence, {mono no kokoro} is a heartfelt appreciation for the deeper, often melancholic, essence of existence.
